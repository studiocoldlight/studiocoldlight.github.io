# Privacy Policy

Coldlight Studio ("we") operates a private application that uploads the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. This policy explains what the application accesses and how data is handled.

## What the application accesses

The application requests a single permission from the channel owner's Google account: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It is used only to upload videos, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to the Coldlight Studio YouTube channel.

## What we collect and store

We do not collect personal data from visitors or from any third party. The only credential the application stores is the OAuth token of the channel owner, saved on the owner's own computer. It is not shared, sold or transmitted to anyone.

## How data is used and shared

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to upload the channel's own videos. We do not share, sell or transfer it to third parties. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.
