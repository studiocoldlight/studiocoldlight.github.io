# Coldlight Studio

Coldlight Studio is an independent YouTube channel that produces narrated documentary-style videos about real cases, news stories, and well-known legends and urban tales.

This website is the home page of the Coldlight Studio application. The application is a private tool that uploads the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner. It offers no service to the public and does not collect any data from visitors.
